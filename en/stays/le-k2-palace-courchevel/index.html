Frequently Asked Questions

01Is the Cospillot piste outside Le K2 Palace manageable for less experienced skiers?

The adjacent Cospillot run is a gentle, wide blue piste leading straight to the central 1850 lift hub, and the hotel's complimentary shuttle drops beginners at the Children's Village in 5 minutes.

02Can guests rent and tune skis directly inside the hotel?

Yes—the in-house Ski Shop handles premium rentals, custom boot fitting, and daily waxing, with valets staging your skis right on the snow.
