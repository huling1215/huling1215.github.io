Frequently Asked Questions

01How far is Hotel Hermitage from the Cervinia lifts, and do guests walk uphill in ski boots?

Though just ~300 m from the Cretaz slopes, the hotel sits on a quiet hillside; its on-demand shuttle drops you right at the lift turnstiles in under 2 minutes.

02Does the hotel ski room have heated boot dryers and storage for personal skis?

Yes—every room gets a dedicated heated ski locker with boot-warming pegs inside the monitored ski room.
