01

How Do Piste Connectivity, Glacier Elevation, and Season Length Compare Across the Three?

Savoie and Haute-Savoie host Europe's flagship ski domains, yet each skis very differently. Les 3 Vallées unites Courchevel, Méribel, and Val Thorens across 600 km of seamless lift-linked runs with over 80% above 1,800 m. Tignes–Val d'Isère links 300 km rising to the 3,456 m Grande Motte glacier, holding snow reliably from late November into early May. Chamonix spreads distinct sectors—Brévent-Flégère, Grands Montets, Le Tour, and Les Houches—along one dramatic valley linked by buses and rail rather than pistes, crowned by the 3,842 m Aiguille du Midi.

  • Les 3 Vallées (600 km linked): Pure ski-to-ski valley hopping ideal for mixed-ability groups
  • Tignes–Val d'Isère (300 km linked): High 1,850–2,100 m village bases with strong late-season glacier snow
  • Chamonix (valley sectors): Iconic Mont Blanc views and steep freeride lines suited to confident riders

02

How Do Lodging Styles, Après-Ski Dining, and Non-Skier Activities Differ?

Courchevel and Val Thorens in Les 3 Vallées excel at true ski-in/ski-out hotels and apartments, scaling smoothly from sunny Moriond 1650 to chalet-lined 1850. Val d'Isère pairs stone-and-timber charm with vibrant Folie Douce slope parties, while Tignes 2100 emphasizes high-altitude convenience. Chamonix functions as a year-round mountain town packed with gear shops, bakeries, and sightseeing trains for non-skiers.

  • Families & first-timers: Courchevel 1650 or Méribel offer gentle cruisers and doorstep ski schools
  • Spring & freeride chasers: Tignes–Val d'Isère holds winter conditions well into March and April
  • Short breaks & sightseeing: Chamonix sits just ~75 min from Geneva Airport with rich off-snow excursions

Frequently Asked Questions

01For a 4-to-5-day trip, should I buy a local valley pass or the full Les 3 Vallées pass?

Beginners spending their first days on greens/blues save ~20% on a local valley pass, while intermediates planning daily cross-valley tours get strong value from the full 3 Vallées pass.

02Can all three French resorts be visited cleanly on a standard Schengen visa?

Yes—all three lie inside France; arriving at Geneva Airport works seamlessly on a Schengen visa via standard airport transfers.
