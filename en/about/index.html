About PowderClub

An editorial ski travel platform focused on verified global mountain data and slopeside trip planning.

01

What we build

Planning an international ski trip often means cross-referencing trail maps, transfer timetables, and hotel distances across multiple languages. PowderClub standardizes mountain specs and slopeside lodging details into one consistent guidebook.

02

How we verify data

Elevation figures, operating months, and lift walking distances are checked against official resort bulletins, satellite measurements, and on-snow editorial visits, with the verifier and date shown on every page.
