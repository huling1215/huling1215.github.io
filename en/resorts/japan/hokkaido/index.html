  1. Home /

Spanning Niseko United, Rusutsu, and Hoshino Tomamu, Hokkaido receives relentless dry Sea of Japan powder alongside gated tree runs, floodlit night skiing, and natural onsen resorts.

Sapporo New Chitose Airport fans out to three distinct hubs: four-mountain Niseko United with lively dining, tree-skiing powerhouse Rusutsu, and family-oriented Hoshino Resorts Tomamu with all-suite towers and indoor wave pools.

Frequently Asked Questions

01How do I choose between Niseko, Rusutsu, and Tomamu for a first Hokkaido ski trip?

Choose Niseko for four linked mountains and dining variety, Rusutsu for uncrowded glades, or Tomamu for family suites and non-ski winter attractions.

02Which months balance deep powder and clearer visibility in Hokkaido?

January through mid-February brings frequent powder cycles, while late February to mid-March pairs a deep base with sunnier Mt. Yotei views.

03Can Niseko and Rusutsu be combined in a single ski itinerary?

Easily—the two resorts sit 40–45 minutes apart by road, making a 6-night split stay between Rusutsu and Niseko seamless.
