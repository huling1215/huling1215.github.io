Naeba Ski Resort

  1. Home /
  2. Resorts /
  3. Japan /
  4. Niigata Yuzawa Region /
  5. Naeba Ski Resort

Anchored beneath 1,789 m Mt. Takenoko in Yuzawa with an 889 m vertical drop and 24 runs. Linked to Kagura via the 5.5 km Dragondola and reachable from Tokyo via Joetsu Shinkansen to Echigo-Yuzawa plus a 45-minute bus ride.

  • The 5.5 km Dragondola spans forested valleys to link Tashiro and Kagura under the joint Mt. Naeba pass
  • Joetsu Shinkansen connects Tokyo Station to Echigo-Yuzawa in 75–85 minutes for efficient 3–4 day getaways
  • Naeba Prince Hotel wings stretch directly along the base slopes with immediate access to gondolas and night skiing
  • Balances wide base beginner slopes with steep World Cup racing pistes and natural mogul faces

Family & Beginner Suitability

Kids' Care & Family Notes

Right outside Wings 4 and 6 sit the Pandaruman kids ski school zone and Waku-Waku Family Snowland, and elementary-school children ride lifts free under Seibu Prince's Kids Free program.

Beginner Terrain Notes

Beginners should practice along the broad lower slopes served by High-Speed Lifts No. 4 and No. 5 right in front of the hotel before riding Gondola No. 2 toward steeper mid-mountain faces.

Frequently Asked Questions

01Should I buy the Naeba-only pass or the Mt. Naeba joint pass covering Kagura and Tashiro?

Beginners and day-one skiers only need the Naeba area ticket; confident blue-run riders on calm days should upgrade to the Mt. Naeba joint pass to ride the Dragondola over to higher-elevation Tashiro and Kagura snowfields.

02How do I transfer from Echigo-Yuzawa Shinkansen Station to Naeba Ski Resort?

Registered Naeba Prince Hotel guests can ride the pre-booked complimentary hotel shuttle from Echigo-Yuzawa East Exit (~40–45 min), while day visitors and flexible travelers can board the public Minami-Echigo express bus (~45–50 min) from the same terminal.
