British Columbia Whistler Region

  1. Home /
  2. Resorts /
  3. Canada /
  4. British Columbia Whistler Region

Two hours north of Vancouver Airport along the scenic Sea-to-Sky Highway, British Columbia’s Whistler Blackcomb links two massive peaks via the Peak 2 Peak Gondola around a vibrant pedestrian village on the Epic Pass.

With a low 675 m pedestrian village and over 1,600 m of vertical climbing into alpine bowls and glaciers, Whistler Blackcomb blends comfortable base elevations with vast terrain for beginners through experts.

Frequently Asked Questions

01Is Whistler Village or Upper Village (Blackcomb Base) better to stay in?

Whistler Village puts dining and dual gondolas outside your door, while quieter Upper Village hosts luxury resorts right beside Blackcomb’s kids ski school—just a 10-minute stroll apart.

02Do I need to rent a car from Vancouver Airport to Whistler?

No car needed—Whistler is completely walkable and hotel parking is pricey; direct airport coaches or private Sea-to-Sky transfers drop right at hotel lobbies.

03Where should we ski at Whistler Blackcomb on snowy or foggy days?

Drop below treeline into Emerald Forest, Creekside cruisers, or Blackcomb’s sheltered mid-mountain runs where evergreens provide sharp contrast.
