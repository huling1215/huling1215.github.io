Frequently Asked Questions

01Can guests staying in Lech ski over to St. Anton and back in a single day?

Yes—ride through Zürs via the Flexenbahn cableway into Alpe Rauz and St. Anton; simply start your return lift sequence before 14:30 to beat final connections.

02Does Lech's daily lift-ticket cap affect hotel guests during peak season?

The cap targets day-trippers to protect piste space; overnight guests staying in Lech–Zürs hotels receive guaranteed lift-pass access through their hotel or local ticket desks.
