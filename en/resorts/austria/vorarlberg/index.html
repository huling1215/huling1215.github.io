Vorarlberg Lech-Zürs Region

  1. Home /
  2. Resorts /
  3. Austria /
  4. Vorarlberg Lech-Zürs Region

Anchored by Lech Zürs on the western flank of Ski Arlberg, Vorarlberg pairs over 300 km of interconnected alpine runs and the famous White Ring circuit with daily ticket caps and refined ski-in/ski-out hotels.

Abundant western Arlberg snowfall and the Flexenbahn gondola link let guests warm up on sunny Lech cruisers, tour the 22 km White Ring loop through Zürs and Zug, or cross over into St. Anton.

Frequently Asked Questions

01How does staying in Lech Zürs feel different from St. Anton?

Lech Zürs feels quieter and sunnier with wide intermediate bowls and daily capacity management, while sharing the exact same Ski Arlberg lift network as livelier St. Anton.

02How long is the transfer from Zurich Airport to Lech Zürs?

Rail from Zurich Airport to Langen am Arlberg takes roughly 2 hours 15 minutes, followed by a 15–20 minute bus or hotel shuttle up to Zürs and Lech.

03Can intermediate skiers complete the White Ring circuit in one day?

Confident intermediates complete the clockwise White Ring in 3–4 hours at a relaxed pace with plenty of time for a mountain hut lunch.
