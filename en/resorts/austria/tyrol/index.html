Tyrol Austrian Alps

  1. Home /
  2. Resorts /
  3. Austria /
  4. Tyrol Austrian Alps

Home to St. Anton, Kitzbühel, and Ischgl, Austria’s Tyrol combines direct ÖBB rail access, high-capacity heated chairlifts, classic mountain huts, and cross-border alpine mileage.

Easily reached from Innsbruck, Munich, and Zurich airports, Tyrol spans steep freeride bowls in St. Anton, cobbled heritage and rolling cruisers in Kitzbühel, and high-altitude cross-border laps between Ischgl and Swiss Samnaun.

Frequently Asked Questions

01How do St. Anton, Kitzbühel, and Ischgl compare by skill level?

Pick St. Anton for expert terrain, Kitzbühel for scenic intermediate cruisers and medieval charm, or Ischgl for high-altitude snow reliability.

02Can I ski Tyrol comfortably by ÖBB train without renting a car?

Very easily—St. Anton and Kitzbühel have mainline rail stations right in town, and Landeck-Zams connects to Ischgl via frequent valley buses.

03Which multi-resort passes work across Tyrol ski resorts?

Kitzbühel and Ischgl partner with Ikon Pass, while St. Anton (Ski Arlberg) offers partner lodging access for Epic Pass holders.
