Mt Hutt Ski Area

  1. Home /
  2. Resorts /
  3. New Zealand /
  4. Canterbury Southern Alps /
  5. Mt Hutt Ski Area

Towering above the Canterbury Plains with a 2,086 m summit and 683 m vertical drop just 90 minutes from Christchurch Airport, Mt Hutt pairs high-altitude snow reliability with wide open bowls and 5 lifts across 365 hectares.

  • 2,086 m summit and 683 m vertical drop overlooking the expansive Canterbury Plains
  • Fast Nor'west Express and Summit Six 6-seater chairlifts maximize laps across 365 hectares
  • Only ~90 minutes from Christchurch Airport and ~35 minutes from Methven village lodging
  • Part of NZSki alongside Coronet Peak and The Remarkables, supporting Three Mountain and Ikon passes

Frequently Asked Questions

01Should I stay in Methven or Christchurch when skiing Mt Hutt?

Base in Methven (~35 min away) for 2+ ski days to skip long mornings and react easily to weather holds; stay in Christchurch for single-day coach trips.

02Is Mt Hutt prone to wind holds, and how do I check before driving up?

Because it sits exposed at 2,086 m, strong northwest gales can trigger wind holds—always check the 06:30 Snow Report before heading up the access road.
