Gangwon-do Ski Region

  1. Home /
  2. Resorts /
  3. South Korea /
  4. Gangwon-do Ski Region

Clustering Yongpyong, High1, Phoenix Pyeongchang, and Vivaldi Park east of Seoul, Gangwon-do pairs KTX rail access with extensive floodlit night skiing, terrain parks, and spacious resort condos.

Stretching along the Taebaek Mountains, Gangwon-do offers distinct personalities: Olympic steeps at Yongpyong, summit beginner cruisers at High1, freestyle parks at Phoenix Pyeongchang, and late-night sessions near Seoul at Vivaldi Park.

Frequently Asked Questions

01Which Gangwon-do ski resort is friendliest for first-time skiers?

High1 Resort’s gentle 4.2 km Zeus summit green run lets beginners ride the gondola to the peak and cruise all the way down; Vivaldi Park is easiest for quick trips from Seoul.

02How do travelers get from Incheon Airport to Yongpyong or Phoenix Pyeongchang?

Ride seasonal direct ski shuttle coaches from Incheon Airport terminals, or connect via Seoul Station onto the KTX Gyeonggang Line to Pyeongchang or Jinbu in roughly 95 minutes.

03What is the difference between Korean resort condos and hotel towers?

Resort condominiums offer multi-room floor-heated layouts with kitchenettes for families or groups, while hotel wings provide traditional daily housekeeping and lobby dining.
