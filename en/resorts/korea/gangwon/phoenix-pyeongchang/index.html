Phoenix Pyeongchang Snow Park

  1. Home /
  2. Resorts /
  3. South Korea /
  4. Gangwon-do Ski Region /
  5. Phoenix Pyeongchang Snow Park

Set on Mt. Taegisan in Bongpyeong-myeon, Pyeongchang, Phoenix Snow Park hosted the 2018 Winter Olympic freestyle skiing and snowboarding events. Features a 400 m vertical drop, 18 runs, 10 lifts, and the signature Extreme Park.

  • Legacy 2018 Winter Olympic venue for freestyle skiing and snowboard halfpipe and slopestyle
  • Home to the renowned Extreme Park with progressive kickers, rails, and wave features
  • Ride the 8-passenger gondola to the summit and cruise the 2.2 km Panorama trail to the base
  • Located just ~15 minutes by shuttle from Pyeongchang KTX Station on the Gyeonggang Line

Frequently Asked Questions

01How do I transfer from Seoul Station to Phoenix Pyeongchang via KTX?

Ride the Gyeonggang Line KTX from Seoul or Cheongnyangni to Pyeongchang Station (~90 min), then board the complimentary 15-minute resort shuttle—note that Pyeongchang Station is closer than Jinbu.

02When do the terrain park and night skiing operate at Phoenix Pyeongchang?

Extreme Park lines typically phase open from late December onward, while floodlit night sessions run roughly 18:30–22:00+ depending on daily grooming schedules.
