High1 Resort

  1. Home /
  2. Resorts /
  3. South Korea /

  5. High1 Resort

Set across mountain ridges in Jeongseon, Gangwon State, featuring a 1,340 m main summit, 660 m vertical drop, 18 trails, and three 8-passenger gondolas. Known for its 4.2 km Zeus summit-to-base green cruiser tailored to beginners.

  • Beginners can ride enclosed gondolas to the revolving summit cafe and cruise the 4.2 km gentle Zeus green trail to the base
  • Three 8-passenger high-speed gondolas and six chairlifts link Mountain Top, Valley Top, and three ski houses seamlessly
  • Wide groomed pistes with hands-free RFID gates alongside FIS-homologated Apollo and Victoria racing slopes
  • Complemented by High1 Water World indoor-outdoor thermal pools, Snow World family sledding park, and resort dining arcades

Family & Beginner Suitability

Kids' Care & Family Notes

Mid-mountain Snow World provides snow rafting, family tubing lanes, and heated igloo lounges, while non-skiing relatives can ride the gondola to view the Taebaek mountain range.

Beginner Terrain Notes

Unlike mountains that reserve peaks for experts, High1 carved the gentle Zeus I–III green route right from 1,340 m—after practicing braking at the base carpet on day one, beginners can enjoy full summit-to-base runs on day two.

Frequently Asked Questions

01What transit options connect central Seoul or Incheon Airport to High1 Resort?

During winter, pre-book seasonal direct ski shuttle buses from Incheon Airport or central Seoul stops (Hongdae, Myeongdong, Jamsil; ~3–3.5 hours), or ride the Taebaek Line train from Cheongnyangni Station to Gohan/Sabuk Station followed by a 10-minute complimentary resort shuttle.

02How does High1 compare in terrain with nearby Yongpyong Resort, and can I visit both?

Yongpyong shines for steeper racing pitches and fast KTX access, whereas High1 stands out for its summit beginner cruisers and three warm gondolas; sitting ~85 km apart (~75 minutes by car), both resorts pair well on a 4-to-5-day Gangwon trip.
