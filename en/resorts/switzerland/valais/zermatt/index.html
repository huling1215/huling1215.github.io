Anchored by the 3,883 m Klein Matterhorn glacier station, delivering a 2,263 m vertical drop across 360 km of linked Swiss-Italian pistes above a car-free village reached directly by SBB and MGB rail.

  • Ride the Matterhorn Glacier Ride 3S cable car to 3,883 m for an uninterrupted 25 km descent back to town

  • Car-free village at 1,620 m preserves crisp alpine air with electric taxis and the historic Gornergrat cogwheel railway
  • Sunnegga, Gornergrat, and Rothorn sectors offer sun-drenched cruising and mountain dining facing the Matterhorn pyramid

Family & Beginner Suitability

Kids' Care & Family Notes

Wolli Beginner Park at Sunnegga features sheltered magic carpets and gentle learning terrain, while children under age 9 ski free across the lift network with a Wolli Card.

Beginner Terrain Notes

Early-stage skiers should build edge control at Sunnegga's Wolli Park and wide Riffelberg blues on Gornergrat before attempting high glacier crossings or steeper valley-return trails.

Frequently Asked Questions

01How do I reach car-free Zermatt by train from Zurich or Geneva Airport?

Both Zurich (ZRH) and Geneva (GVA) airports have integrated SBB rail terminals requiring just one easy change at Visp onto the Matterhorn Gotthard Bahn (3h30m–3h50m total); drivers park at Täsch and ride a 12-minute shuttle train.

02What should I watch out for when skiing across the border into Cervinia, Italy?

Choose the International Ski Pass, carry your passport, and monitor afternoon wind advisories at the 3,480 m Testa Grigia border saddle so you catch the final return lifts before closure.
