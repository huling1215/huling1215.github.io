Switzerland's expansive interconnected ski domain linking Verbier, Nendaz, and Veysonnaz up to the 3,330 m Mont Fort glacier across 410 km of pistes and freeride itineraries.

  • A single 4 Vallées pass unlocks 410 km of runs and 80+ lifts stretching to the 3,330 m Mont Fort glacier
  • Renowned for marked off-piste yellow itinerary routes such as Tortin and Mont Gelé for seasoned freeriders
  • Seamless rail-to-gondola connection from Geneva Airport via Martigny and Le Châble straight into Verbier
  • Vibrant après-ski terraces around Place Blanche pair with acclaimed mountain dining and luxury chalets

Frequently Asked Questions

01Do skiers need a mountain guide for Verbier's yellow itinerary routes?

Yellow itineraries are patrolled for avalanche safety but left ungroomed with steep moguls and variable snow; carry avalanche gear and hire a certified local guide for your first descent.

02How does the train journey from Geneva Airport to Verbier work with ski bags?

Ride SBB from Geneva Airport to Martigny and change onto the St-Bernard Express to Le Châble (~2 hours), where the gondola steps away lifts you directly to Verbier.
