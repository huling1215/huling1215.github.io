St. Moritz Corviglia

  1. Home /
  2. Resorts /
  3. Switzerland /
  4. Graubünden Engadin St. Moritz /
  5. St. Moritz Corviglia

Crowning the sunny Upper Engadin valley from 1,720 m to 3,057 m Piz Nair, Corviglia delivers 155 km of broad, sun-drenched pistes and Olympic downhill runs.

  • High sunshine count across southeast-facing Engadin slopes with famously crisp, carpet-smooth grooming
  • Ascend to 3,057 m Piz Nair for panoramic Engadin lake views and classic Olympic downhill pitches
  • Links via the Engadin regional pass with Corvatsch and Diavolezza for over 350 km of varied skiing
  • Slope-side sun terraces pair with frozen-lake polo, horse racing, and historic Cresta Run heritage

Frequently Asked Questions

01Is St. Moritz Corviglia well suited to intermediate carving skiers?

Yes—roughly 60% of Corviglia consists of wide, sun-lit intermediate cruising runs groomed to high precision for confident carving.

02Does the St. Moritz ski pass cover Corvatsch and neighboring Engadin mountains?

Multi-day Upper Engadin passes include Corviglia, Corvatsch, Diavolezza, and valley ski buses; Friday Snow Night sessions require a separate night ticket.
