High in Italy's Aosta Valley with a 2,050 m snow-sure village base, rising to 3,480 m at Plateau Rosa to link across the border with Zermatt into the 360 km Matterhorn Ski Paradise.

  • A 2,050 m village base and 3,480 m glacier slopes sustain reliable, dry snow conditions from November into May
  • Add the International Ski Pass to ski over Plateau Rosa into Zermatt across 360 km of Swiss-Italian terrain
  • Cruise the iconic 11 km Ventina descent from 3,480 m down to 2,050 m on wide, sweeping red-blue fall lines
  • Enjoy dramatic south-face Matterhorn views with noticeably friendlier Italian hotel tariffs and rifugio lunch prices than Zermatt

Family & Beginner Suitability

Kids' Care & Family Notes

Beginner Terrain Notes

Cervinia's wide, forgiving fall lines extend all the way to Plan Maison and Plateau Rosa, letting progressing beginners enjoy high-alpine mileage in windproof layers.

Frequently Asked Questions

01Which ski pass do I need to ski from Cervinia into Zermatt, and what time cutoffs apply?

Purchase the Cervinia–Zermatt International Ski Pass (or a single-day upgrade), carry your passport, and watch ridge wind alerts so you ride the Plateau Rosa return lift before 15:30–16:00 closures.

02Is Turin or Milan Malpensa Airport better for reaching Breuil-Cervinia?

Long-haul travelers often prefer Milan Malpensa (MXP, ~2h45m drive) for direct flight frequency, while European connections into Turin (TRN) cut the road transfer down to ~1h50m.
