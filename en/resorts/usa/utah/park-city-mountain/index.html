Frequently Asked Questions

01Should visitors stay near Historic Main Street or Canyons Village in Park City?

Main Street excels for walkable dining and Town Lift access, while Canyons Village offers modern slopeside resorts and slightly shorter drives from SLC; free town buses connect both bases frequently.

02Do Park City Mountain and neighboring Deer Valley share the same lift pass?

No—Park City Mountain is on Epic Pass and welcomes both skiers and snowboarders, whereas adjacent Deer Valley is on Ikon Pass and ski-only; free local buses link the two areas in ~10 minutes.
