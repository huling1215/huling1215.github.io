Combines scenic Glacier Express panoramic rail travel with 5 ski-in/ski-out nights at Riffelalp Resort 2222m plus 1 Swiss rail-hub night, bundling a 4-day Zermatt–Cervinia International Ski Pass and Swiss rail transfers.

  • Sleep 5 nights car-free at 2,222 m for immediate morning piste access followed by 35°C outdoor pool soaks facing the Matterhorn
  • Use your 4-day International Ski Pass to ski from 3,883 m across the border into Cervinia, Italy for pasta and espresso
  • Cap the ski week riding the panoramic Glacier Express across snow-draped viaducts and alpine passes toward Zurich

What's included / Not included

What's included

  • 5 consecutive nights in a Chalet Classic Double Room at Riffelalp Resort 2222m (breakfast & Spa 2222m access included) plus 1 night at a 4-star Zurich Airport or Chur rail hotel
  • 4-day Zermatt–Cervinia International Ski Pass covering all Swiss and Italian lifts, Gornergrat Bahn, and Matterhorn Glacier Ride 3S
  • Reserved panoramic coach seat aboard the Glacier Express (Zermatt–Chur sector) plus 2nd-class Swiss rail airport transfer pass
  • Round-trip porter luggage and ski-bag transfer between Zermatt Bahnhof and Riffelalp Resort 2222m

Not included

  • International round-trip flights to Switzerland and Schengen visa application fees
  • Personal ski/snowboard rental gear, private mountain guides, and optional 3-course lunch service aboard the Glacier Express
  • Single-room supplement, lunches/dinners, and mandatory alpine winter sports rescue insurance

Frequently Asked Questions

01Do Swiss SBB trains and the Glacier Express charge extra for ski bags?

Standard SBB/MGB trains and the Glacier Express include dedicated end-of-coach ski racks at no extra charge, or you can use SBB station-to-airport luggage forwarding so you travel hands-free.

02What skiing or snowboarding level gets the best experience from this Zermatt package?

Comfortable intermediates who link parallel turns on blue/red runs will get full value—able to cruise sunny Gornergrat pistes and cross the 3,480 m border pass onto Cervinia's wide Italian boulevards.
