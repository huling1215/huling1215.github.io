Frequently Asked Questions

01When do most trails at Beidahu open for the winter season?

Preview skiing typically begins in mid-November, with core intermediate, advanced, and glade zones widely open from mid-December through late February, followed by milder spring snow in early March.

02How do I transfer from Changchun Longjia Airport to Beidahu?

Take the seasonal airport express coach (around 110 minutes) directly to partner hotels, or ride the 18-minute intercity train from Longjia Station inside the terminal to Jilin Station followed by a 65-minute taxi.

03Is it better to base in Wānaka or Queenstown when skiing Cardrona?

Wānaka sits roughly 35 minutes from the Cardrona base without crossing the Crown Range switchbacks and pairs naturally with Treble Cone, while splitting nights between Wānaka and Queenstown works well for multi-resort trips.

04Do self-drive visitors need snow chains for the Cardrona mountain access road?

Snow chains are mandatory whenever road advisories are active along the 10 km gravel access road; to skip fitting chains, park free at the Pine Trees lot at the bottom for the mountain shuttle or book town-to-mountain bus transport.

05How do airport and high-speed rail transfers work for Changbaishan Wanda Resort?

Guests flying into Changbaishan Airport (NBS) board complimentary resort coaches right outside arrivals for a 20-minute drop-off at partner hotels; travelers arriving at Changbaishan High-Speed Rail Station can book an 85-minute resort shuttle or taxi, while conventional trains also stop at Songjianghe Station 14 km away.
