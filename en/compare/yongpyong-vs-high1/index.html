Terrain Profile & Who Fits Where

South Korea · Pyeongchang, Gangwon

Yongpyong Resort

Set on Daegwallyeong Ridge in Pyeongchang, Gangwon Province, offering a 758 m vertical drop across 28 runs and 14 lifts. Former international alpine racing venue situated roughly 20 minutes from Jinbu KTX Station.

Terrain breakdown
  • Beginner 25%
  • Intermediate 45%
  • Advanced 30%

Beginner Terrain Notes: Beginners should start on the wide, gentle Yellow and Pink base runs to build braking control before taking the gondola up to the 5.6 km Rainbow Paradise cruiser.

Kids' Care & Family Notes: Adjacent to Dragon Plaza sit a fenced snow sledding park, indoor playroom, and multilingual ski school, letting families handle gear rental, warm-up breaks, and lesson drop-offs in one plaza.

South Korea · Jeongseon, Gangwon

High1 Resort

Set across mountain ridges in Jeongseon, Gangwon State, featuring a 1,340 m main summit, 660 m vertical drop, 18 trails, and three 8-passenger gondolas. Known for its 4.2 km Zeus summit-to-base green cruiser tailored to beginners.

Terrain breakdown
  • Beginner 40%
  • Intermediate 35%
  • Advanced 25%

Beginner Terrain Notes: Unlike mountains that reserve peaks for experts, High1 carved the gentle Zeus I–III green route right from 1,340 m—after practicing braking at the base carpet on day one, beginners can enjoy full summit-to-base runs on day two.

Kids' Care & Family Notes: Mid-mountain Snow World provides snow rafting, family tubing lanes, and heated igloo lounges, while non-skiing relatives can ride the gondola to view the Taebaek mountain range.
