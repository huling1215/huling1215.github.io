Terrain Profile & Who Fits Where

Switzerland · Valais · Zermatt

Zermatt Matterhorn Ski Paradise

Anchored by the 3,883 m Klein Matterhorn glacier station, delivering a 2,263 m vertical drop across 360 km of linked Swiss-Italian pistes above a car-free village reached directly by SBB and MGB rail.

Terrain breakdown
  • Beginner 21%
  • Intermediate 62%
  • Advanced 17%

Beginner Terrain Notes: Early-stage skiers should build edge control at Sunnegga's Wolli Park and wide Riffelberg blues on Gornergrat before attempting high glacier crossings or steeper valley-return trails.

Kids' Care & Family Notes: Wolli Beginner Park at Sunnegga features sheltered magic carpets and gentle learning terrain, while children under age 9 ski free across the lift network with a Wolli Card.

France · Haute-Savoie · Mont-Blanc Valley

Chamonix-Mont-Blanc

Alpine mountaineering capital beneath Mont Blanc, pairing the 3,842 m Aiguille du Midi cable car and Vallée Blanche glacier run with Brévent-Flégère, Grands Montets, and Le Tour.

Terrain breakdown
  • Beginner 18%
  • Intermediate 52%
  • Advanced 30%

Beginner Terrain Notes: Beginners thrive on wide blue/green runs at Le Tour and Les Houches; upper Brévent and Grands Montets suit confident intermediates and experts.

Kids' Care & Family Notes: Le Tour / Balme and tree-lined Les Houches offer forgiving family terrain, while the in-town Le Savoy nursery slope sits steps from hotels.
