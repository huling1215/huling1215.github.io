Terrain Profile & Who Fits Where

New Zealand · Wānaka, Otago

Cardrona Alpine Resort

Set between Wānaka and Queenstown for Southern Hemisphere winter skiing from June to October, pairing an 1,860 m summit and 600 m vertical drop with wide alpine basins, Soho Basin terrain, and progressive parks.

Terrain breakdown
  • Beginner 20%
  • Intermediate 35%
  • Advanced 45%

Beginner Terrain Notes: Unlike resorts where summits are strictly steep, Cardrona's Chondola carries novices straight up to wide, forgiving green trails high above the treeline.

Kids' Care & Family Notes: Base buildings house a licensed early-childhood nursery alongside full-day Skiwees and Lowriders camps for ages 5–14 right beside base cafés.

New Zealand · Queenstown, Otago

Coronet Peak & The Remarkables

Pairing Queenstown's two NZSki fields on one pass from June to September, combining The Remarkables' 1,943 m sunny alpine bowls with Coronet Peak's roller-coaster cruisers and floodlit night sessions 25–40 minutes from ZQN Airport.

Terrain breakdown
  • Beginner 22%
  • Intermediate 38%
  • Advanced 40%

Beginner Terrain Notes: First-timers thrive on The Remarkables' sunny, gentle learner slopes before progressing to Coronet Peak's rolling blue runs overlooking Lake Wakatipu.

Kids' Care & Family Notes: The Remarkables funnels its sunny bowls and enclosed learner conveyors directly back to one central base building, making family meetups effortless.
