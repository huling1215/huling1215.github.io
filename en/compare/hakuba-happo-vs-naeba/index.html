Terrain Profile & Who Fits Where

Japan · Hakuba, Nagano

Hakuba Happo-One Snow Resort

Anchoring Nagano's Northern Alps with a 1,071 m vertical drop and an 8 km top-to-bottom run. Former Winter Olympic downhill host pairing sustained groomed cruisers with Hakuba's alkaline hot springs.

Terrain breakdown
  • Beginner 30%
  • Intermediate 50%
  • Advanced 20%

Beginner Terrain Notes: Upper Happo ridges pitch steeply and form afternoon moguls; beginners should stay on Sakka and lower Shirakaba green/blue runs until confident on sustained red pitches.

Kids' Care & Family Notes: Sakka Base offers flat access, English-speaking international ski school kids groups, and a snow play zone—making nearby Wadano lodges practical for families with school-age children.

Japan · Yuzawa, Niigata

Naeba Ski Resort

Anchored beneath 1,789 m Mt. Takenoko in Yuzawa with an 889 m vertical drop and 24 runs. Linked to Kagura via the 5.5 km Dragondola and reachable from Tokyo via Joetsu Shinkansen to Echigo-Yuzawa plus a 45-minute bus ride.

Terrain breakdown
  • Beginner 40%
  • Intermediate 30%
  • Advanced 30%

Beginner Terrain Notes: Beginners should practice along the broad lower slopes served by High-Speed Lifts No. 4 and No. 5 right in front of the hotel before riding Gondola No. 2 toward steeper mid-mountain faces.

Kids' Care & Family Notes: Right outside Wings 4 and 6 sit the Pandaruman kids ski school zone and Waku-Waku Family Snowland, and elementary-school children ride lifts free under Seibu Prince's Kids Free program.
