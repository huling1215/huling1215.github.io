Terrain Profile & Who Fits Where

France · Savoie · Tarentaise Valley

Courchevel · Les 3 Vallées

Uniting Courchevel, Méribel, and Val Thorens across 600 km of ski-linked pistes reaching 3,230 m, anchored by stepped ski-in/ski-out villages spanning 1,100 m to 1,850 m in Savoie.

Terrain breakdown
  • Beginner 25%
  • Intermediate 50%
  • Advanced 25%

Beginner Terrain Notes: Sunny, extra-wide green pistes and designated Easy Rider ZEN zones above the Ariondaz gondola in Courchevel 1650 make progression calm and intuitive.

Kids' Care & Family Notes: Dedicated fenced Children's Villages and themed adventure greens in Courchevel 1850 and Moriond 1650 pair smoothly with ESF English-speaking kids programs.

Austria · Tyrol · Arlberg

St. Anton am Arlberg

Gateway to Austria's interconnected Ski Arlberg domain, rising from a 1,304 m rail-served village to the 2,811 m Valluga summit across 300 km of groomed pistes and 200 km of marked off-piste routes.

Terrain breakdown
  • Beginner 43%
  • Intermediate 40%
  • Advanced 17%

Beginner Terrain Notes: St. Anton grades steeper than many resorts—beginners do well lapping gentler Gampen or Rendl mid-mountain slopes and riding the gondola back down when afternoon valley runs turn mogulled.

Kids' Care & Family Notes: Enclosed kids snow gardens and warm lunch rooms sit beside both the Nasserein and Galzig base terminals, staffed by Ski School Arlberg instructors.
