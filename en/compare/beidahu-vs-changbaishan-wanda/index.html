Terrain Profile & Who Fits Where

China · Jilin

Beidahu Ski Resort

Set in Yongji County, Jilin with an 870 m vertical drop across 64 runs and 11 lifts. Calm winter winds preserve dry snow, paired with base-area ski-in/ski-out hotels and a dedicated kids camp.

Terrain breakdown
  • Beginner 20%
  • Intermediate 55%
  • Advanced 25%

Beginner Terrain Notes: First-timers should spend days 1–2 on the South Zone magic carpets and Starlight green run before riding the gondola to upper Qingyun Boulevard for gentle long-distance cruising.

Kids' Care & Family Notes: The second floor of the main service center houses an indoor kids club and fenced magic-carpet learning slope, offering full-day small-group lessons with lunch for ages 4–12.

China · Fusong, Jilin

Changbaishan Wanda International Resort

Sheltered in a three-sided valley at 41°N in Fusong County, Jilin, reaching 1,210 m with a 395 m vertical drop across 43 runs and 7 lifts, paired with ski-in/ski-out hotels and volcanic hot springs.

Terrain breakdown
  • Beginner 47%
  • Intermediate 32%
  • Advanced 21%

Beginner Terrain Notes: Nearly half of the trail network caters to beginners—spend day one practicing speed control on the Town or Guosong magic carpets before riding the Gentleman Gondola for wide, gentle green cruisers.

Kids' Care & Family Notes: Both the Town West and Guosong base centers host fenced kids magic-carpet slopes and full-day camp classrooms with lunch for ages 4–12, within easy walking distance of village dining and Hannashan Hot Springs.
