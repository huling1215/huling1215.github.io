Frequently Asked Questions

01Which Schengen visa applies for Zermatt or St. Anton, and how should visitors pair Swiss rail passes?

Switzerland, France, Austria, and Italy all belong to the Schengen Area—apply through the country where you spend the majority of nights, which also covers cross-border skiing from Zermatt into Cervinia. Travelers landing in Zurich or Geneva can buy the 1-month CHF 150 Swiss Half Fare Card for 50% off SBB rail legs and select mountain excursions.

02Is transferring trains in Switzerland difficult with a 23 kg ski bag, and how does SBB luggage shipping work?

SBB InterCity and regional trains feature low-floor entries and upright ski racks where ski bags under 23 kg ride free; travelers preferring hands-free transfers can book SBB station-to-station luggage shipping (standard 2-day delivery window) alongside a valid rail ticket.

03How does European Alpine piste color grading differ from Asia and North America?

Swiss, Austrian, and Italian resorts typically use Blue (easy), Red (intermediate), Black (steep), and Yellow (ungroomed Ski Routes) rather than green circles, while French resorts like Les 3 Vallees retain Green-Blue-Red-Black groomed grades. Off-piste terrain outside marked pistes is unpatrolled and requires transceiver, probe, shovel, and a certified mountain guide.
