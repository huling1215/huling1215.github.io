Frequently Asked Questions

01Why do some self-described ski resorts hotels still require a 15-minute morning shuttle queue?

Sprawling resort complexes often place annex wings or chalets 400 m+ down sloped roads from the main gondola; always check the exact flat walking distance from your specific building's ski locker door to lift gates.

02Are true ski-in/ski-out hotels equally suitable for first-time skiers?

Verify that the trail outside the locker room is a green run or magic-carpet zone rather than an intermediate pitch—properties like Hyatt Regency Changbaishan, Thaiwoo Ski Hotel, and Naeba Prince open directly onto gentle beginner snow.
