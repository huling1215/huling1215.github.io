Frequently Asked Questions

01Can someone with a few indoor dry-slope or simulator sessions join an off-piste powder camp directly?

Not recommended—outdoor mountain runs add variable snow textures, rollers, and lift transitions; spend 3 days solidifying parallel turns on groomed green and blue runs at Beidahu, Thaiwoo, or High1 before entering deep snow.

02Is Korea's High1 or Japan's Naeba better suited for an overseas novice ski trip?

High1 features a gentle 4.2 km summit-to-base green cruiser that builds confidence rapidly, while Naeba sits roughly 2 hours from Tokyo via Shinkansen plus bus with softer natural snow right outside Naeba Prince Hotel.

03Should I buy wide powder skis or a directional board before my initial deep-snow trip?

No need to purchase ahead—renting 95–110 mm waist skis or a powder board locally on storm days lets you experience float without oversized airline baggage logistics.
